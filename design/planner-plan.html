If you fall behind: the plan does not silently pile tasks up. Missed tasks roll forward as overdue, and once there are more than five the planner offers to re-spread the remaining topics across the weeks left.

ON TRACK, STATED PLAINLY

A green pill when you are ahead, amber when you are behind, with the number of tasks either way — and a projected finish from your actual pace, not the plan's.

COVERAGE, NOT JUST PROGRESS

62% overall hides that paediatrics has not started. The topic bars show where the gap is, which is what an exam plan is really for.

THE PLAN CAN BE CHANGED

Adjust the pace, edit topics, pause. A plan nobody can reshape gets abandoned the first busy week — so re-spreading is offered automatically.
