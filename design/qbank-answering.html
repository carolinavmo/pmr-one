WHY B IS RIGHT

Heel pain that worsens with loading and is now present at rest, with pain reproduced by mediolateral compression of the calcaneus — the squeeze test — points to a calcaneal stress fracture. Early radiographs are often normal, so MRI is needed if suspicion stays high.

WHY THE OTHERS ARE NOT
APlantar fasciopathy — focal plantar-medial tenderness and first-step pain, not diffuse tenderness with a positive squeeze test.
CBaxter nerve entrapment — medial plantar heel pain, often burning; not painful on calcaneal compression.
DAchilles tendinopathy — posterior pain at the insertion or within the tendon, with loading.
EFirst MTP osteoarthritis — great-toe pain on push-off, not heel pain.
