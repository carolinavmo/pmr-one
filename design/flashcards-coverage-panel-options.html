Percentage of the collection — six ways

"How far through am I" as the frame. It answers a real question — but it moves slowly, so each version pairs it with something that moves today.

RECOMMENDED

1 + 3. The coverage bar as the panel, with the next milestone underneath. One number for where you are, one that moves every session.

THE HONEST CAVEAT

A percentage of the collection changes by under 1% on most days. On its own it is a poor thing to open the page with — it needs the session beside it, which every version here keeps.

DEFINE IT ONCE

"Known" must mean one thing across the panel, the tiles and the topic pages — review state with an interval of 21 days or more. And say it somewhere, or people will argue with the number.
