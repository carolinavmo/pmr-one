Which loading protocol is first-line for mid-portion Achilles tendinopathy?
Progressive tendon loading — starting with isometrics, then heavy slow resistance

Twelve weeks of eccentric or heavy slow resistance training is the best-supported first-line treatment. Load is progressed by symptom response, not by the calendar, using pain during and 24 hours after exercise as the guide.

CLINICAL PEARL

Pain during loading is allowed — up to about 5/10, provided it settles within 24 hours. Telling patients this is often what makes the programme work.
