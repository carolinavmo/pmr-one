  • 2Star — outline by default, amber when favourited. Tappable on the card; no need to open the tool.

WHAT WAS WRONG

  1. Titles cut mid-word — "FIM - Functional Independenc…", "IADL - Lawton-Brody Instrume…". Descriptions too.
  2. The abbreviation is repeated in every title: "BI - Barthel Index", "mRS - Modified Rankin Scale".
  3. The same icon on every card — 40px that says nothing.
  4. Favourites duplicate the cards below with no sign they are the same tool.
  5. The sidebar shows the library tree while you are in Clinical Tools — no way to jump between categories.
  6. No filtering or sorting, and nothing on the card about length, time or population.

WHAT CHANGED

  1. Sidebar becomes the tool index — all, favourites, categories with counts, recently used, and "by population" for the clinical question.
  2. Cards carry real metadata: items, time, population. Same data as the tool page header.
  3. Category colour on the abbreviation chip and the section dot, so a card is recognisable out of context.
  4. Favourites in a tinted band, and the same tools keep a filled star in their category below — duplicate, but visibly linked.
  5. Search hints: name, abbreviation, or what it measures. "Try: barthel, BI, balance, stroke severity".
  6. Cards / List toggle — a dense table for people who know what they want.
