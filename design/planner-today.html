1TODAY, NOT A MONTH

The page opens on three tasks and 45 minutes, with a button that starts them. The month grid is now one view inside Calendar.

2TASKS COME FROM THE PLATFORM

Each task is a library page, a flashcard session or a question set — tagged, timed, and startable in one click. Not a generic to-do list.

3THE RAIL IS THE PLANNER

The library tree is gone. In its place: today, this week, overdue, done, then your plans with their progress.

4THE WEEK AT A GLANCE

Seven days with what was done, what was missed and what is planned — today outlined. A rest day says "rest day", not nothing.

5PLANS, NOT TASKS ALONE

Board exam, rotation, weekly routine — each with a deadline, progress and whether you are on track. Tasks are generated from them.

6NUMBERS THAT MOVE

Due today, this week's fraction, streak and hours studied — instead of a 0% ring and an empty goal.
