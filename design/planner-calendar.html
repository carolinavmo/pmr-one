CHIPS, NOT DOTS

Each task is a chip in its plan's colour, with the type and the size. Completed ones are struck through and faded rather than hidden — the month should show effort.

A DAY PANEL, NOT A POPUP

Clicking a day fills the panel on the right, with the tasks, their estimates, and ▶ Start this day. No modal to dismiss.

FILTER BY PLAN

Three plans, three colours, each toggleable — plus a workload control that caps how many tasks a day the generator will schedule.
